## Summary

StepTrack Pro tracks your steps and derived health metrics **entirely on your
device**. We (the developer) do not operate any servers, do not collect your
data remotely, and do not sell or share your data with third parties.

## What data the App collects

| Data | Purpose | Where it's stored |
|---|---|---|
| Step counts (via the device's step-counter/step-detector sensors) | Daily step tracking, history, goals | Locally on-device (Room database) |
| Profile info you enter (name, age, height, weight, gender) | Personalizing distance/calorie estimates | Locally on-device (Room database) |
| App settings (goal, units, theme, notification preferences) | Remembering your preferences | Locally on-device (DataStore) |

The App does **not** collect: your location, contacts, photos, browsing
history, or any data from other apps. The App does **not** require an account
or sign-in.

## Permissions the App requests, and why

- **Activity Recognition** (`ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`) — required by Android to
  read step-counter/step-detector sensor data.
- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) — used to show the ongoing
  tracking notification and goal/reminder alerts.
- **Foreground Service / Foreground Service Health** — lets step counting
  continue reliably while the app is in the background or the screen is
  locked.
- **Boot Completed** — restarts step tracking automatically after your
  device reboots.
- **Ignore Battery Optimizations (optional, user-initiated)** — you can
  choose to exempt the App from OEM battery management so background
  tracking isn't interrupted; this is never requested silently.

## Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your step or health data to any third
party, ad network, or analytics service, because the App does not include
any such integrations.
